Lumi Letters is built for children. It works fully offline and does not collect, store, or share any personal information — from you or your child.

No data collection No third-party ads No tracking No account needed Works offline

Privacy Policy

Information we collect

None. Lumi Letters does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal data such as names, emails, photos, contacts, or location. We have no login and no user accounts.

Where your child’s progress is stored

Learning progress (letters completed, friends earned, settings) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves the device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising & analytics

There are no third-party ads, no behavioural advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs in this app.

Children’s privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

This app is designed for children and is compliant with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU GDPR provisions for children. Because we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing to disclose to third parties and no data ever leaves the device.

External links & purchases

The app contains no advertising links. Any settings or external links (e.g. a privacy page) are placed behind a parent gate. Optional purchases, if available, are handled securely by Apple’s App Store; we never see your payment details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date below.
