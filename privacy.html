Your family’s privacy

Privacy Policy 隱私權政策

🛡️ Last updated 2026-06-06 · 最後更新

The short version: we collect nothing.

Lumi Letters is built for children. It works fully offline and does not collect, store, or share any personal information — from you or your child. This policy covers the iOS editions on the App Store and the Android editions on Google Play alike.Lumi 字母星球為兒童打造，完全離線運作，不收集、不儲存、不分享任何個人資訊。本政策同時適用於 App Store(iOS)與 Google Play(Android)上的版本。

01 Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal data such as names, emails, photos, contacts, or location. There is no login and no user account.沒有。本 app 不會要求、收集或傳送任何個人資料，包括姓名、電子郵件、照片、通訊錄或位置，也沒有登入或帳號。

02 Where your child’s progress is stored

Learning progress and settings are saved only on your own device. They never leave the device and are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.學習進度與設定只儲存在您的裝置上，絕不離開裝置，也不會傳給我們或任何第三方；刪除 app 即移除。

03 Advertising & analytics

There are no third-party ads, no behavioural advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs in this app.本 app 沒有任何第三方廣告、行為式廣告，也沒有分析或追蹤 SDK。

04 Children’s privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

This app is made for children and complies with the U.S. COPPA and the EU GDPR provisions for children. Because we collect no personal information at all, nothing is ever disclosed to third parties and no data leaves the device.本 app 為兒童設計，符合美國 COPPA 與歐盟 GDPR 兒童規範。由於完全不收集個人資訊，沒有任何資料分享給第三方，也沒有資料離開裝置。

05 External links & purchases

The app contains no advertising links. Any external links are placed behind a parent gate. Optional purchases, if available, are handled securely by Apple’s App Store on iPhone and iPad, and by Google Play on Android; we never see your payment details, and we never receive your Apple Account or Google Account. A purchase can be restored on a new device by signing in with the same account you bought it with.app 內無廣告連結；任何外部連結皆在家長關卡之後。若有選購項目，在 iPhone 與 iPad 上皆由 Apple App Store 安全處理，在 Android 上則由 Google Play 處理，我們不會看到您的付款資訊，也不會收到您的 Apple 帳號或 Google 帳號。換新裝置時，用原本購買的帳號登入即可回復購買。

06 Permissions

The app asks for no sensitive permissions on either platform — no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications. On Android it never asks you to grant a runtime permission; it uses internet access only so that Google Play can process a purchase or show the optional rating prompt, plus vibration for gentle haptic feedback. Audio playback and speech run entirely on the device.本 app 在 iOS 與 Android 上都不要求任何敏感權限——沒有相機、麥克風、定位、通訊錄、照片或通知。Android 版從不要求您授予任何執行階段權限，網路連線僅供 Google Play 處理購買與顯示選用的評分提示，另使用震動提供輕微的觸覺回饋；音訊播放與語音合成皆於裝置本機運作。

07 Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Email hourstag.app@gmail.com.若政策變更，將於此頁公布更新版本並標註新日期。有問題請來信 hourstag.app@gmail.com。

For parents: children's privacy at a glance 給家長：兒童隱私重點

This is a summary of the policy above. It adds no new practice and does not change it. 以下是上方政策的重點整理，沒有新增或變更任何做法。

What information is collected from my child?

None. The app does not ask for, collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information from children or parents. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Learning progress and settings, and anything your child creates or enters in the app, stay only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. 沒有。App 不會向孩子或家長要求、蒐集、傳送、販售或分享任何個人資訊；沒有帳號、不需登入。學習進度、設定，以及孩子在 App 內建立或輸入的內容，都只存在你的裝置上，刪除 App 時一併移除。

Advertising and tracking

No advertising of any kind, no behavioral advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not track your child across other companies' apps or websites. 沒有任何廣告、沒有行為式廣告、沒有數據分析、沒有追蹤，也沒有會蒐集資料的第三方 SDK；App 不會跨其他公司的 App 或網站追蹤孩子。

COPPA and GDPR-K

Because no personal information is collected from children, no parental consent for data collection is needed, nothing is disclosed to third parties, and the app is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children's provisions of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K). Purchases and links that lead outside the app are meant for parents and are handled as described above. 由於不向兒童蒐集任何個人資訊，因此不需要為資料蒐集取得家長同意，也沒有任何資料揭露給第三方；本 App 依美國《兒童線上隱私保護法》（COPPA）與歐盟 GDPR 兒童條款（GDPR-K）設計。購買與離開 App 的連結是給家長使用，處理方式如上方政策所述。

Your rights as a parent

We hold no personal information about you or your child, so there is nothing on our side to review, export or delete. You can erase everything the app stored at any time by deleting the app from the device. If you have any question or request about your child's privacy, contact us and we will reply. 我們沒有保存任何關於你或孩子的個人資訊，因此在我們這端沒有可供查閱、匯出或刪除的資料。你隨時可以從裝置刪除 App，以清除 App 儲存的所有內容。若對孩子的隱私有任何問題或要求，歡迎與我們聯絡，我們會回覆。
