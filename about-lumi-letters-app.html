What it is

Lumi Letters Lite is an English alphabet app for children aged three to seven. Kids hear each letter's name and sound, trace uppercase and lowercase letters along stroke-order guide lines, and collect letter friends.

  • Hear each letter's name and sound
  • Trace uppercase and lowercase letters with stroke-order and crosshair guides
  • Planet adventures with listening, first-sound, ending-sound, middle-vowel and letter-order questions
  • Learning dashboard with four skills, plus a printable PDF worksheet
  • No ads, works offline

Who it is for

  • Parents of children aged 3–7 who are starting English letters and sounds
  • Families who want a calm, ad-free app that does not need an internet connection
  • Parents who want to see strengths and gaps (letter recognition, letter order, sound awareness, picture reading)

How it is paid for

The download is free. The letter A with all its activities and the first planet are playable without paying. One purchase unlocks all 26 letters and 14 planets. There is no subscription and no recurring charge; the purchase sits behind a parent gate. Check the current price on the store page.
