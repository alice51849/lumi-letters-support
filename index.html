Why parents trust Lumi

Built for little learners who can’t read yet 為還看不懂字的小小學習者而生

Lumi Letters turns the 26 letters into a gentle world of play — listen, trace with proper stroke order, match cases, and collect a friend for every letter. Everything is spoken aloud, so children can learn all on their own.

🎧

Hear every letter

Each letter and word is spoken in a clear, friendly voice, and every instruction is read out loud — perfect for pre-readers.每個字母與單字都用清楚的語音念出，連題目指令也會念，看不懂字也能玩。

✏️

Trace the right way

Guided stroke order with numbered starts shows children exactly how each letter is written.有號碼順序的筆順引導，讓孩子知道每個字母怎麼寫。

🐻

Collect 26 friends

Finish a letter’s challenge and a brand-new letter friend joins the journey — 26 to find.完成字母挑戰就能獲得一隻字母夥伴，共 26 隻等你收集。

💌 We’d love to hear from you

Questions, feedback, or a feature wish? Email us — a real person reads every message, usually within a day or two. 有問題、建議或想要的功能？來信給我們，每一封都有真人閱讀，通常一兩天內回覆。

Frequently asked

Questions, answered 常見問題

Is it safe for my child?對孩子安全嗎？+
Completely. There are no third-party ads, no pop-ups, no links out, and no data collection of any kind. Settings sit behind a parent gate.100% 安全。沒有第三方廣告、彈窗或外部連結，也不蒐集任何資料；設定有家長關卡保護。
My child can’t read yet — can they play?還看不懂字也能玩嗎？+
Yes! The whole game is picture- and sound-based and every instruction is spoken aloud, so children can play independently.可以！整個遊戲以看圖、聽音為主，所有指令都會念出來，孩子能自己玩。
Does it need the internet?需要網路嗎？+
No — Lumi Letters works fully offline. Great for car rides and flights.不需要，完全離線可玩，車上、飛機上都適合。
What ages is it for?適合幾歲？+
Designed for preschoolers and early learners, on both iPhone and iPad, with a calm and encouraging pace.專為學齡前幼兒設計，iPhone 與 iPad 皆支援，步調溫和、鼓勵為主。
Is it bilingual?有中英文嗎？+
Yes. The interface follows your device language (English by default, Chinese on Chinese devices) and can be switched anytime in Settings.有。介面依裝置語言顯示（預設英文、中文裝置顯示中文），也可在設定隨時切換。
How is progress saved & how do I restore purchases?進度怎麼存？如何恢復購買？+
Progress is stored only on your device. If a purchase didn’t appear, reinstall and tap “Restore Purchases”, or email us and we’ll help right away.進度只存在您的裝置上。若購買未顯示，重新安裝後點「恢復購買」，或來信我們會立即協助。

Your family’s privacy

Privacy Policy 隱私權政策

🛡️ Last updated 2026-06-06 · 最後更新

The short version: we collect nothing.

Lumi Letters is built for children. It works fully offline and does not collect, store, or share any personal information — from you or your child.Lumi 字母星球為兒童打造，完全離線運作，不收集、不儲存、不分享任何個人資訊。

    No data collection不收集資料
  • ✓
    No third-party ads無第三方廣告
  • ✓
    No tracking or analytics無追蹤與分析
  • ✓

01 Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal data such as names, emails, photos, contacts, or location. There is no login and no user account.沒有。本 app 不會要求、收集或傳送任何個人資料，包括姓名、電子郵件、照片、通訊錄或位置，也沒有登入或帳號。

02 Where your child’s progress is stored

Learning progress and settings are saved only on your own device. They never leave the device and are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.學習進度與設定只儲存在您的裝置上，絕不離開裝置，也不會傳給我們或任何第三方；刪除 app 即移除。

03 Advertising & analytics

There are no third-party ads, no behavioural advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs in this app.本 app 沒有任何第三方廣告、行為式廣告，也沒有分析或追蹤 SDK。

04 Children’s privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

This app is made for children and complies with the U.S. COPPA and the EU GDPR provisions for children. Because we collect no personal information at all, nothing is ever disclosed to third parties and no data leaves the device.本 app 為兒童設計，符合美國 COPPA 與歐盟 GDPR 兒童規範。由於完全不收集個人資訊，沒有任何資料分享給第三方，也沒有資料離開裝置。

05 External links & purchases

The app contains no advertising links. Any external links are placed behind a parent gate. Optional purchases, if available, are handled securely by Apple’s App Store; we never see your payment details.app 內無廣告連結；任何外部連結皆在家長關卡之後。若有選購項目，皆由 Apple App Store 安全處理，我們不會看到您的付款資訊。

06 Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Email hourstag.app@gmail.com.若政策變更，將於此頁公布更新版本並標註新日期。有問題請來信 hourstag.app@gmail.com。
