❓ FAQ / 常見問題

Is it safe for my child? / 對孩子安全嗎？

Yes — 100%. No third-party ads, no pop-ups, no links out, and no data collection. Settings sit behind a parent gate.
是的，100% 安全。無第三方廣告、無彈窗、無外部連結、不收集資料，設定也有家長關卡保護。

Does it work offline? / 可以離線玩嗎？

Yes. Everything works without internet — great for trips.
是的，完全離線可玩，旅途中也適合。

What age is it for? / 適合幾歲？

Designed for ages 3–7, including pre-readers — every instruction is spoken aloud.
專為 3–7 歲設計，包含還看不懂字的孩子——每個指令都會念出來。

My child can’t read yet — can they play? / 還看不懂字也能玩嗎？

Absolutely. The whole game is picture- and sound-based, with spoken instructions, so children can play independently.
當然可以。整個遊戲以看圖、聽音為主，並會念出指令，孩子能自己玩。

How is progress saved? / 進度怎麼儲存？

Only on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
只存在您的裝置上，刪除 app 即會移除。

How do I restore purchases? / 如何恢復購買？

If a purchase didn’t appear, reinstall and tap “Restore Purchases”, or email us and we’ll help.
若購買未顯示，重新安裝後點「恢復購買」，或來信，我們會協助。
