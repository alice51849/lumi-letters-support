Lumi Letters Lite: ABC Kids

Children can hear letter names and sounds, trace uppercase and lowercase forms, and practise through playful letter activities. Letter A is the free starting point.

What the app does

Children can hear letter names and sounds, trace uppercase and lowercase forms, and practise through playful letter activities. Letter A is the free starting point.

Data and privacy

Learning progress and settings stay on the device. There are no third-party ads, analytics or tracking, and no child account is required.

Purchase

The remaining visible letters and activities open through an optional one-time purchase handled by Apple. A parent or guardian should use the purchase and external-link controls.
